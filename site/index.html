Protection shifts the curve downward.

It does not eliminate the warming-driven trajectory. Under SSP5-8.5, with-levee exposure reaches 11.09% in 2100 — approximately 870 million people under the fixed-population assumption.

The historical reduction is modelled exposure avoided, not observed damage avoided. Protection standards are held fixed. These are hazard-driven changes, not projections of population growth.

About these map data

The full-resolution grid is 43,200 × 21,600 cells (30 arcseconds). Zoomed-out layers use averaged overviews; at close zoom the original Float32 grid is loaded without smoothing. Exact-cell queries always use the original grid. Depths ≤0.1 m are transparent on the map but remain queryable. The optional 2020 population overlay is a coarse visual reference; only flood-depth layers and cell queries use the original 30″ grid.
